08 — Key Insights for Ulta Beauty
Market Opportunity
01 / 06
10.9%
J-Beauty's share of total TikTok views
J-Beauty's Gap Is a Marketing Problem,
Not a Demand Problem
J-Beauty holds just 10.9% of combined TikTok views across tracked brands — despite decades of proven product heritage. This underrepresentation reflects a structural gap in TikTok content investment, not a lack of consumer interest. For Ulta, this signals untapped potential rather than exhausted demand.
Retailer Precedent
02 / 06
16.8B
views — SKIN1004, Ulta's top K-Beauty performer
K-Beauty's Ulta Success Was Built on TikTok
— J-Beauty Is at the Same Starting Line
SKIN1004 (16.8B views), Medicube (14.2B), ANUA (6.6B) built their U.S. consumer awareness almost entirely through TikTok virality. Kiss Me (4.68B) and CANMAKE (1.28B) are approaching a comparable inflection point — the same one K-Beauty passed 2–3 years ago.
Growth Signal
03 / 06
+110M
views added by Yunth in 90 days alone
J-Beauty's Growth Leaders Show Real Momentum
— Not Small-Base Artifacts
Yunth added +110M views in 90 days with a 193M base — meaningful absolute growth. ReFa (+88M) shows similarly strong gains. These reflect genuine consumer discovery of J-Beauty on TikTok, driven by organic content around skincare routines and beauty devices.
Portfolio Strategy
04 / 06
Next
J-Beauty as natural portfolio extension
J-Beauty Deepens What K-Beauty Built
— A Natural Next Chapter for Ulta
Consumers who discovered skincare through K-Beauty are increasingly exploring J-Beauty's minimalist routines, precision tools, and barrier-first formulations. CANMAKE, ReFa, and Yunth expand the assortment without cannibalizing existing K-Beauty performance.
Urgency

K-Beauty's 90-day growth advantage over J-Beauty
The First-Mover Window Is Closing
— Time Is a Strategic Variable
K-Beauty added 8.26B new TikTok views in 90 days vs. J-Beauty's 0.54B. Retail shelf presence and TikTok momentum reinforce each other. Brands that enter early shape the category narrative; those who wait inherit a more competitive landscape.
